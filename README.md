# HeatingAirDublin

Sample website for Heating & Air Conditioning, an HVAC contractor at 7136 Village Pkwy, Dublin, CA.

Live site: https://gordonjaychan.com/HeatingAirDublin/

- `index.html`: page content
- `style.css`: design (brand colors are at the top)
- `script.js`: "Open now" status (Pacific time), mobile menu, footer year
- `images/`: favicon (add the client's photos here once they send them)

The page has `noindex` set so it stays out of Google until the business approves it.
